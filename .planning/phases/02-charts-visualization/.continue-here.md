## Critical Anti-Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Severity | Prevention Mechanism |
|---------|-------------|----------|---------------------|
| `useRegisterSW()` tuple destructured as boolean | `needRefresh` from `useRegisterSW()` is a `[value, setter]` tuple; destructuring it as a plain boolean made it always truthy (G-02-1) | advisory | Always destructure PWA register hooks per their documented tuple shape; see commit `6eef7b9` and `src/components/UpdatePrompt.tsx` |
| Fixed-position FAB inside bottom-nav band | Log button used `bottom-6` (24px) which sat inside the fixed bottom nav's 64px band and was painted over by the nav's higher z-index (G-02-2) | advisory | When adding fixed-position controls on screens with a bottom nav, offset clear of the nav's full height + z-index; see commit `2d12acf` and `src/components/Charts/MetricChart.tsx` |
