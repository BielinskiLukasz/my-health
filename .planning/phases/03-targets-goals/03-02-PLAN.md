<objective>
Build the streak and personal-best engines on top of Plan 03-01's target/pace foundation, and
surface both on the Dashboard tile and the chart screen. Streaks answer "did today qualify
against the active target" (a hard daily check, distinct from Plan 03-01's 7-day pace/color
logic) and walk backward with no grace day. Personal bests answer "is this the best value ever
seen for this metric" across all 5 target-eligible metrics plus sleep/steps/water/heartRate's
natural PB semantics — independent of whether a target is even set.

Purpose: DASH-03 and TARG-05 require a visible, correct streak; PB-01/02 require detection that
covers pre-existing history, not just entries logged after this feature ships.
Output: extended `targetCalcs.ts` (streak functions), new `personalBest.ts` (PB detection, TDD),
`useStreakData`/`usePersonalBestData` hooks, and Dashboard/chart wiring for both.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Historical metric data (read) -> personalBests cache (write) | The retroactive scan reads across up to years of existing weight/sleep/steps/water/heartRate history and writes only into the new, separate personalBests table. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-04 | Denial of Service | usePersonalBestData retroactive full-table scan | medium | mitigate | Scan runs once per metric and caches the result in `personalBests`; subsequent loads only compare new entries against the cached best, never re-scanning the full table on every render. |
| T-03-05 | Tampering | Retroactive PB scan write path | high | mitigate | `detectPersonalBest` is read-only over `weights`/`sleepEntries`/`stepEntries`/`waterEntries`/`heartRates`; it only ever writes to the separate `personalBests` table, never mutating a source metric table (satisfies the prohibition above). |
| T-03-06 | Tampering | calculateStreak walk-back on malformed/gapped history | medium | mitigate | The walk-back loop treats any missing or unparseable entry as an immediate break (D-16) rather than throwing, and is hard-bounded to 365 iterations (RESEARCH Example 3) to prevent a runaway loop. |
</threat_model>
