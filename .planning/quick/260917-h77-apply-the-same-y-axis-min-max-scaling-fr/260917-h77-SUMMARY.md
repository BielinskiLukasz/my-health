**Dashboard mini sparklines now scale their Y-axis to the 14-day min/max for weight/heart-rate/temperature (mirroring the full-chart G-02-5 fix), and MetricTile's "not logged today" tile drops its date line and status pill in favor of a taller (60px) sparkline.**

## Accomplishments
- Sparkline.tsx now imports `YAxis` and `getYAxisDomain`, rendering `<YAxis domain={yAxisDomain} hide />` so weight/heartRate/temperature sparklines scale to their 14-day data range instead of a 0-anchored default
- Sparkline.tsx accepts an optional `height` prop (default 40), driving `ResponsiveContainer` and both loading/empty placeholder states via inline `style={{ height }}`
- MetricTile.tsx's variant b (not logged today) no longer renders the last-logged-date line or "Not logged today" status pill; the unused `lastDate` destructure and `formatShortDate` import were removed
- All 6 metric tile `<Sparkline>` call sites now pass `height={60}` uniformly
