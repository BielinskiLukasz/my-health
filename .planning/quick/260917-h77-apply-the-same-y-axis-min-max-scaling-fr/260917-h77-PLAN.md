<objective>
Two related visual fixes to the Dashboard's mini metric tiles:

1. The 14-day Sparkline (`src/components/Charts/Sparkline.tsx`) currently renders its `<LineChart>` with no `<YAxis>` at all, so Recharts defaults to a 0-anchored scale — flattening real trends for weight, heart rate, and temperature, exactly the problem G-02-5 already fixed for the full-size chart in `MetricChart.tsx`. Apply the same `getYAxisDomain(metric)` utility (already built and tested in `src/utils/chartDomain.ts`) to the Sparkline.
2. `MetricTile.tsx`'s variant "b" (metric not logged today, showing the last known value) currently renders a last-logged-date line and a "not logged today" status pill above the Sparkline, both purely decorative and consuming vertical space that could make the sparkline itself more readable. Remove that line and pill from variant b only, and grow the Sparkline's rendered height into the freed space, applied uniformly to all 6 metric tiles.

Purpose: Consistent, honest Y-axis scaling across every chart surface in the app (full chart + dashboard sparkline), and a more legible/taller trend line on the Dashboard's most common tile state.
Output: Updated `Sparkline.tsx` (YAxis domain wiring + configurable height prop) and `MetricTile.tsx` (variant b simplified, taller Sparkline passed in for all 6 tiles).
</objective>
